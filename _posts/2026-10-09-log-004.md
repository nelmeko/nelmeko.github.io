Music has always been a part of my life. School, work, playing games, or chilling, music is always there to accompany it. I've been thinking for a while now how cool it is that someone had an idea in their head that only they can think of, and produce said idea into sound which then we perceive as music. Then having people actually like your music and relating to it due to the feeling it evokes or because of what the lyrics say. 

My favorite type of music genre depends on the day and mood. But, I have always had a love for electronic music. I remember watching this video on youtube about counter strike source. It was an animation and to this day I rewatch it from time to time to keep in touch with my younger self. But, in the video, the creator used 2 bands that I like. Pendulum and Deftones. Bro was putting me on when I was only like 8 years old. Pendulum is gas. Literally my favorite DnB band/group. Every single song they have put out, I have listened to. My favorite song from them is hard to choose but Plasticworld is for sure up there. You should give it a listen. 

As I have grown older, I have found groups such as Crystal Castles and artists such as Pastel Ghost. Oh my god. Their music is so good. I love this type of music. Speeding late at night with the windows down having their music on blast. All that is in front of me is the lit up road. I have also found another artist, Cynthoni. Her most recent album is my favorite. Songs such as "Even Further Down the Drain" and "Cynthoni of Flames" are amazing. Her music is good and when she released a remix of Pendulum's "Hold your Colour", I was ecstatic. Not only does this new artist that I found also knows about Pendulum but they remixed one of their best songs, I came to some sort of idea. 

What if nely dove into making music himself? I've seen my friend Joe make music himself and heard my other friends such as Gurgs, Gloom, and Ethan want to create music or at least mess around in FL Studio. I want to create art. Not because I want to be famous. But to put something out there, that my mind created. Something only my mind can think of. Maybe someone out there can come across it and listen and decide whether my music is worthy to give a listen to. To properly critique it and/or share with people and enjoy the type of sound my head produces. 

This is only one aspect of my plan on creation. Music is the biggest one though. Music can convey emotions better than any other type of medium in my opinion. So, when I have some extra free time, I want to sit down and learn more about creating music and how to use DAWs (ty joe!) and see if i can drop a song or even an album if I'm going crazy. My type of music genre will be electronic but I'm not sure just yet which sub genre it will be. So stay tuned! :)
