## Mustang GT

I am not a big fan of making stupid purchases. Financial literacy is very important to me. It was time for me to buy a new car due to work and university. I was looking at Civics and such but my mom told me, "if you die tomorrow, you would've never had the pleasure of driving a cool car." So, I was set on buying something stupid for the first time. 

My first choice has always been the F80 BMW M3. I love that car. Not because of tiktok or instagram trends but because the lines on the car were beautiful. Nice sedan with 450HP. I found one in Glendale. Red bucket seats, manual transmission which was buttery smooth, and active exhaust. I was so excited, the first car I went to go see was the one I was going to get as my 2nd car. But the dealership denied me. They told me I was a first time buyer. Whatever that means. 

I was angry. The car I have always admired as a child was denied. I went to another dealership and saw a lot of Mustangs lined up and in my head I told myself, "anybody can buy a mustang, I dont want them." But something clicked when I saw a blue Mustang GT. S550 body with the 5.0 badge. I remember customizing a GT on the Ford website when I was 16. I told myself, "you know what, fuck it." 

I had it delivered home and when it came in, the guy dropping it off told me, "damn man, a GT? You got a girlfriend to go with this beauty?" I said no. We laughed and he said enjoy the ride. I felt nothing when the car was in my driveway. I looked at it and just sent a pic to my friends and went inside for a bit. But something was calling me towards it. 

I told my dad to come with me for a quick test of the car to see how it feels. Hit the push to start and heard the 5.0 Coyote come to life. "Oh shit" I thought to myself. I slowly reversed out and drove towards the street. I saw the gears change from 1st to 3rd then to 5th. I realized right there the type of car I just bought. I drove it carefully due to the common stereotype of Mustang drivers. I let my dad drive it and he immediately put his foot down turning into the main road and he almost crashed it like an idiot.  

We went home and I still had a weird feeling towards the car. I took it out that same night. Went to my favorite street which goes for miles with no police activity. Roads are perfectly paved and clear. I slowly started to put my foot down and felt the 460HP through the entire cabin. 

The last push I did with my car was in an area with no cameras. I came to the stop sign and looked all over the place to make sure the coast was clear. No lights, nobody crossing, I put my foot down. My entire back fell back into my seat, the front of the car lifted and I saw 4th gear - 90 miles per hour. I had 6 more gears left. 

I went home with the biggest smile on my face. At 23 years old, I had a monster waiting for me in the driveway. I daily drive it too which has taught me to stay in control and not to do stupid things on the road. This car is incredibly dangerous. With the smallest amount of pressure on the pedal, the car wants to go. This car is always waiting to be let loose. I try my very best to not speed. Many other drivers on the road tempt me, they catch up to me and drop their windows and start beeping. I usually say no because I cant die yet. Other things need to be taken care of first. But this car, I love it. 

I plan on soon to go to every single major city or viewpoint in California with the Mustang and take pictures of it with a Polaroid and save them into a scrapbook. I love my Mustang.

But when I do race someone, just try and catch me, im only stock too :)
